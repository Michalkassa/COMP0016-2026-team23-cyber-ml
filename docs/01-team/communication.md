## Professional courtesy (module rules)

- No emails to supervisors, academics, TAs or industry guests **after 6 pm or at weekends**. Schedule the send for working hours.
- Don't message staff on Teams expecting a reply; send an email.
- Email academics **24 h in advance** to request a meeting.
- Negotiate meeting times with the partner. If they reject **2+ slots in a row**, notify Mehran Allybaccus (SAT) **and** Prof Dean Mohamedally.
- Cameras on in online meetings with external partners (unless a reasonable adjustment is filed). Dress appropriately for external meetings.
- Senior guests may visit labs at short notice.
